Why every month

With AI, attacks got faster. Last month's safety is this month's open door.

Attackers use AI and automated tools to hunt for publicly known weaknesses and open entry points. They will not wait until you find them first.

  1. 1
    Known weaknesses are attacked quickly

    When a weakness in a device you use is made public, attackers search for that same device everywhere at once. If you have not fixed it, that is the way in.

  2. 2
    Someone's work adds new entry points

    A test site, a newly signed service, a device with changed settings. Without anyone noticing, they become visible from outside.

  3. 3
    Settings left alone become material for impersonation

    An expiring domain, leftovers from a cancelled service, weak email settings. A fake invoice sent to your business partners goes out in your company's name.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we choose the outside view only?

Yes. We check every month using public information only, and deliver reports you can use whether you fix things yourselves or hire another company.

Will this disrupt our work?

Neither plan touches your devices or systems, so your work is not affected. In the inside view, we only read settings and never change them.

When do we get results?

The first report arrives about two weeks after you apply, then every month. In the inside view, the settings review, interview and training emails happen every six months.

How much does it cost?

There are two plans, the outside view and the inside view, both billed monthly. We give you the price during the free consultation. Fixing what we find, and tests that touch your devices, are quoted separately.

Is cyber insurance enough?

Insurance covers costs and losses after an incident. It does not stop your business from being disrupted. For what is covered (some policies make lost business income an option), please check with your insurer or agent.

Once we have a check, are we safe?

No. New weaknesses keep being found (for example, a serious PHP vulnerability in October 2026). Attackers use AI and their methods keep changing. That is why we check every month and tell you what changed.

Can companies outside Fukui Prefecture apply?

During the pilot period, we are limiting this to companies in Fukui Prefecture.

We are under attack right now. Can you help?

Immediate incident response itself (containing the breach and restoring systems) is outside our scope. To stop the damage from spreading, please contact the police cyber desk or the IPA (Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan) first. After things settle, we can help investigate the cause and run a prevention-focused assessment, so feel free to reach out to us as well.

Can we also ask about using AI inside our company?

Yes. If you want to use AI without sending company information outside, we can introduce the Local AI Pack. If you want to build AI into your work, we can also introduce AI Adoption Support (FDE).
