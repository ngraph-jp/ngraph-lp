FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we ask for help even if we are not comfortable with computers or AI?

Yes. We explain without jargon and you don't need to prepare documents. We work through it while looking at your actual work on site.

What is an FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)?

A form of support where engineers go inside your company and build AI into the work on site.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the task and how far we connect. Consultation and estimates are free. You can also start with a small optional PoC.

What size and type of company is this for?

Mainly mid-sized and small companies, in any industry. You can start with one department's work.

Do we need to replace our existing business systems?

No. We build AI in between the systems, Excel and chat tools you already use.

How do you handle confidentiality?

If you wish, we sign an NDA before starting and agree in advance on the scope of information we handle.
