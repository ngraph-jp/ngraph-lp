Security

Don't expose. Don't break. Don't overshare.

We don't rely on settings alone. Three layers protect you, including a posted page and daily habits.

Setting

Don't expose

HR, payroll, bank and personal-data folders are set so AI cannot read them. We test that it works by naming them on the spot.

Setting

Don't break

Delete and reset commands are blocked. Anything that sends data outside needs a person's OK each time. Inside the working folder, you can say "put it back as it was" to undo.

Posted page and habits

Don't overshare

One page on what may and may not be entered is posted in your office. AI answers are drafts. A person checks numbers and dates.

What you type goes to the AI company's servers. So people decide what goes in.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we sign up if no one on our team knows much about AI?

Yes. That is what this set is for. Just name one person in charge; they do not need to be an expert. Users simply ask in plain Japanese, in the app window they normally use.

Do we need to decide in advance which tasks AI will handle?

No. We set it up first, complete one real task in the training session, and find the next ones as you use it.

Do we need a ChatGPT or Claude subscription?

Yes. The generative AI service is contracted in your company's name. Usage fees are not included in the set. We decide together which one to subscribe to in the first online consultation.

How many PCs can you set up?

Up to five. The PCs being set up must allow software installation.

How long does it take?

About 5-7 business days from start to finish. The actual schedule is set in the first online consultation to suit your business. It can also be split into 1.5-hour sessions per day.
